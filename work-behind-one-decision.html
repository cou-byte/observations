2 · The experience

Before the data could inform anything, they had to move.

Five steps between "the numbers exist" and "we decided." Each step shows what happened, the hidden work it took, and the question the research should answer.

Workflow
Illustrative placeholder
Step 01 / Find

The pieces live in four places, on four schedules.

Who: reading coachTime: an afternoonSystems: 4

Benchmark scores sit in the assessment platform. Attendance and enrollment are in the student information system. Last year's intervention history is a shared spreadsheet. What teachers have noticed is in notebooks and email.

The hidden work

Knowing which system holds which piece, and who can log in to each one, is itself expertise that only a few people on campus have.

Question the research should answer

What work stands between having data and acting on them?

3 · Behind the scenes

What the decision actually drew on.

The data, the systems, the people, and the conditions. In a published story each item traces to the reviewed account; here they are placeholders that show the shape.

Illustrative placeholder

Data

  • Fall benchmark reading scores one point in time
  • Attendance, year to date daily, lagging a week
  • Prior-year intervention history manually maintained
  • Teacher observations unstructured

Systems

  • Assessment platform vendor portal, CSV export
  • Student information system district-managed access
  • Shared spreadsheet owned by last year's coach
  • Email and paper notes no system

People

  • Reading coach did most of the assembly
  • District data specialist granted access, fixed IDs
  • Three third-grade teachers supplied context
  • Principal made the final call

Conditions

  • Benchmark window closed two weeks before the support groups had to start
  • Student IDs formatted differently in two systems
  • One person knew all four systems
What made it harder

Each export needed a separate login and arrived in a different layout. Matching students across files was done by hand. The person who knew how was also teaching.

What made it easier

The data specialist had already built a crosswalk of student IDs for another request and shared it. Teachers were asked one specific question, not for "input."
